Privacy Policy

Last updated: September, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Hotspot Control ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Use of Android VpnService

Our app utilizes the Android VpnService API to provide local DNS proxy and network routing features essential for managing your mobile hotspot connections. We do not use this VPN service to collect, log, or transmit your personal internet traffic, browsing history, or DNS requests to any remote server. All network routing is handled locally on your device.

3. Information Collection and Use

Hotspot Control is designed to respect your privacy. The data we interact with is treated as follows:

  • Location Data: The app requests Coarse/Fine Location permissions and Phone State permissions. This is strictly required by the Android operating system to manage Wi-Fi and Hotspot tethering states. We do not track your physical location or share it with external servers.
  • In-App Purchases: We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and purchases. We do not process or store your payment details directly. RevenueCat collects purchase history and anonymized subscriber IDs to unlock premium features.
  • Advertising Data: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID and usage data to serve personalized ads.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Please review their privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • RevenueCat

5. Data Retention & Security

Your Hotspot configurations and logs are stored locally on your device using a local database. We do not upload your personal usage data to our servers. Because your data is stored locally, uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data will permanently delete your stored configurations.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
